Recipe for melody:

Death of addiction. The body of a dead addict, the ghost of a strangers life. Wreckage stacked, hollow eyes. Empty rebirth: a sinners loop, endless fall, endless climb until judgment's fire. The present made memory. Slow fractured guitar, voice like wind through broken glass. Stuck in yesterday's pain, regret and isolation. Never forgiven, already dead. The jester plays what the wreckage wrote.
